Phaser DevTools

Chrome cannot open DevTools from an extension. Use the steps below.

  1. Open Developer Tools: F12, or Ctrl+Shift+I (Windows/Linux), or ⌘+⌥+I (macOS).
  2. In the DevTools toolbar, open the Phaser panel.

Tip: set a shortcut for this popup under chrome://extensions/shortcuts (e.g. Show Phaser DevTools instructions).
